# Garden Empire world art

Original SVG illustrations authored in this repository with Codex assistance for Garden Empire 0.6. No stock pack, competitor screenshot, external reference-image crop, embedded font or downloaded illustration is included.

- cottage.svg: warm tiled cottage, glazed windows, timber flower box, steps and planting.
- market.svg: striped awning, timber counter, potted bouquets and botanical sign.
- well.svg: stone well, water, bucket, rope and green tiled canopy.
- tree.svg: layered foliage, warm trunk, canopy highlights and fruit.
- pond.svg: shaped bank, water highlights, stones, lily pads and reeds.

Each building texture is rendered by its own interactive world Button. Trees and pond are decorative shared textures. Transparent margins do not contain hidden functional controls. All shapes/gradients are editable SVG source. Native landscape paths and soil geometry are in garden_board.gd and plot.gd. The five crop SVGs in assets/ui are also original, refined in the same palette.

These are project-authored assets; they add no third-party attribution requirement. Existing Godot/bundled-font notices still apply. Trademark clearance and store readiness are not asserted.
