## 2. Product thesis and positioning
Working title Garden Empire: From Seed to Fortune. Cozy, readable, isometric 2D portrait game. Player begins with a small plot, plants, harvests, sells, upgrades, unlocks a flower stand, nursery and greenhouse; over time transitions from gardener to owner of automated botanical businesses. Distinction: visible garden beautification + simple buy-grow-sell decisions + transparent, deterministic market demand + meaningful plant discovery. No gambling, artificial urgency, punitive streaks, or pay-to-win. Game is enjoyable without monetization.

## 3. Player personas and user jobs
A: 18–35 casual player who wants 1–5 minute satisfying breaks. B: cozy decorator who wants visual ownership. C: optimization player who wants efficiency and business progression. These are hypotheses, not measured demographic facts. Support accessible text sizing, reduced motion, sound mute, high contrast, and color-independent status labels.

## 4. Game loop and first-time user experience
0–10s: player sees tiny garden, 20 coins, a highlighted empty plot and one seed; one tap plants. 10–30s: starter plant matures after 8s, visible growth stages, tap to harvest and sell; no modal tutorial walls. 30–60s: afford first meaningful upgrade and show its income impact before purchase. 1–3min: second plant species, choice between faster growth and higher margin, and unlock a small stand. 3–5min: demonstrate offline earning concept and persistent visual improvement. Timing is INITIAL DESIGN TARGET, tune through telemetry. Never block exit or force ad viewing.

Primary loop: choose seed -> spend coins -> plant in available plot -> grow through time -> harvest -> add inventory -> sell -> reinvest -> unlock plots, varieties, shops, helpers. Secondary loop: fulfill orders, decorate, discover variants, upgrade automation. Long loop: expand property, nursery, greenhouse, flower shop, new biomes only after validated retention.

## 5. MVP scope (must actually ship as a coherent playable vertical slice)
One garden scene with 4x4 grid (16 plots); 5 plants: carrot, sunflower, rose, lavender, orchid; 3 growth stages per plant; tap planting/harvesting; plant selection; coin wallet; inventory; sell-one/sell-all; 8 upgrades; 1 flower stand; offline growth; offline stand income with caps; local saves; simple quests (5); a collection book; settings; onboarding; event telemetry abstraction; dev balance/debug panel disabled in release; touch and mouse input; Android export documentation. No server, accounts, multiplayer, auction, real-time price feeds, real-money purchases or live-ops in MVP.

## 6. Economy configuration — illustrative initial tuning, NOT final balance
Starting wallet 20 coins; 4 plots unlocked; 12 plots unlockable via plot upgrade costs [20,35,60,95,150,240,380,600,950,1500,2400,3800]. Plants JSON fields: id, name, seed_cost, growth_seconds, base_sell_value, level_required, visual_key, rarity_tier, xp_reward. Initial examples: carrot 5/8s/9/level1; sunflower 12/20s/24/level1; rose 30/45s/65/level2; lavender 70/90s/155/level3; orchid 180/180s/420/level5. Profit per cycle = sale value - seed cost. Early ROI and payback should be analyzed via simulations, not guessed. Flower stand unlock cost 150 coins; stand automatically sells eligible inventory at configurable intervals (does NOT conjure free inventory); auto-harvester unlocked later via explicit helper upgrade. Upgrade price scaling = round(base_cost * multiplier^level), multiplier stored per upgrade (initial 1.55). Avoid 32-bit overflow; use 64-bit integers, cap at 9e15, format compact values. No floating-point currency accounting. Economic config data externalized and schema validated. Explicitly document any adjustments.

## 7. Plant state machine and time
Plot states EMPTY -> GROWING -> READY -> EMPTY. Each plant has planted_at_utc, maturity_at_utc, species_id, plot_id. Render growth phase as deterministic function of elapsed duration; harvesting only in READY. Never let negative clock deltas reverse growth. Offline progress uses UTC wall clock with cap 8 hours and monotonic in-session clock. Detect implausible clock jumps and limit payout; never silently wipe progress. Reconcile on resume, not per-frame. Stand consumes inventory on tick, respecting available quantity and wallet cap. All state transitions must be atomic; no duplicate harvest, sale, purchase, or offline credit after app resume.

## 8. Player progression
XP from planting/harvest/order fulfillment; unlocks are clearly displayed with level requirements. Early milestone: first seed, first harvest, first profit, first upgrade, first flower stand, first rose, first full garden, first offline return. Collection book stores species discovered and best plant variant. Add 5 non-expiring quests with clear rewards; no daily login penalty. For later phases: demand events are transparent and deterministic, optional plant breeding with discoverable rules (never paid randomized loot), decoration, new garden areas, multiple shops, cloud sync, cosmetics, social sharing.

## 9. UX/art/audio specification
Portrait reference canvas 1080x1920 scalable; respect safe areas and screen sizes 320–600 logical px. Top HUD wallet/level, central garden with clearly selectable plots, bottom seed/inventory/shop navigation; purchase confirmation for expensive upgrades, always show affordability and expected benefit. Isometric tile grid with original stylized sprites, at least 3 plant stages, pleasant growth and coin animations, subtle haptics where supported, sound effects with mute toggle, reduced-motion setting, legible contrast and large touch targets >=44dp. No misleading fake buttons or deceptive ads. Assets original or documented licensed; keep assets manifest with source/license.

## 10. Architecture
Godot 4.x, typed GDScript. Scene tree: Boot, MainMenu, Garden, HUD, SeedPicker, InventoryPanel, ShopPanel, CollectionPanel, SettingsPanel, PauseOverlay. Autoload services: GameState, EconomyService, TimeService, SaveService, EventBus, AnalyticsService, AudioService. Pure modules: PlantRules, UpgradeRules, OfflineRules, CurrencyFormatter, ConfigValidator. Config in res://data/*.json or Resources. Persistence schema versioned with migration support; atomic write via temp file + rename; keep rolling backup; corruption recovery with user-visible message; save after meaningful changes and app background. RNG seed injection for tests. No network dependencies. Separate rendering from economic logic.

## 11. Telemetry and privacy
Define event schema: install_first_open, tutorial_step_completed, seed_planted, plant_harvested, item_sold, upgrade_purchased, stand_unlocked, session_start, session_end, game_resumed, save_error, progression_milestone, settings_changed. Each event: anonymous install id (only if consent and permitted), app version, platform, session id, timestamp, schema version, contextual parameters without PII. Prototype logs locally only; remote analytics opt-in/consent according to applicable rules and platform policy. Never collect precise location, contacts, names or advertising identifiers by default. Track D1/D7/D30 cohort retention, FTUE completion, time-to-first-harvest, session count, median session duration, upgrade conversion, currency sources/sinks, error-free sessions. Define retention by calendar-day cohort consistently; segment by acquisition channel, platform, version.

## 12. Commercial design (POST-validation)
No forced interstitials in prototype. After retention tests, evaluate opt-in rewarded video for doubling a completed offline payout (capped), cosmetic decorations, and a one-time supporter pack. Always give a usable free path; don't sell randomized plant outcomes or manipulate odds. Validate AdMob/mediation SDK platform policy, Apple/Google billing requirements, privacy disclosures, children's audience implications, GDPR/CCPA and consent requirements before adding SDKs. Monetization acceptance gate: no measurable retention or satisfaction regression in controlled testing. Do not add paid features before core-loop validation.

## 13. Research experiments
A/B creative smoke test (no misleading claims): modern garden nursery vs beach resort garden vs medieval herbalist vs fantasy botanical lab. Same underlying proposed gameplay; compare ad creative click-through, landing-page signup intent and cost per qualified interest with randomized distribution. Creative CTR is not retention. Run five moderated usability sessions and >=20 unmoderated prototype tests before polishing. Ask users to describe goals and frustrations; observe rather than lead. Hypotheses: (H1) >70% understand planting unassisted within 15s; (H2) >60% complete first harvest; (H3) >40% voluntarily make 3 purchases/upgrades; thresholds are internal gates, NOT industry statistics. For meaningful D1/D7 retention estimates, recruit hundreds of independent testers and report confidence intervals, not point estimates alone.

## 14. KPIs and gates
Technical gate: zero crash on smoke test; saves survive restart; offline payouts idempotent; no economy-negative balances; >=30 fps on representative midrange Android; all unit/integration tests pass. Prototype gate: 80%+ of observed testers understand first action without help, median first harvest <45s, qualitative fun signal in interviews. Soft-launch goals (hypotheses): D1 >=30%, D7 >=8%, D30 >=2%; compare to platform/region benchmarks and confidence intervals. Do not buy significant installs unless retention, stability and LTV/CAC economics are understood. Metrics should be revised after initial evidence.

## 15. Testing and edge cases
Unit: affordability, exact balance updates, zero inventory, multiple taps same frame, maturity boundary, negative and huge clock deltas, upgrades cap, config malformed/missing, 64-bit currency cap, save version migration, load corruption. Integration: fresh install FTUE, plant/harvest/sell loop, unlock shop, close/reopen midgrowth, background/resume repeatedly, device offline, app killed during save, orientation/resolution scaling, audio/reduced motion, Android touch, keyboard/mouse desktop. Simulation: 10k virtual player runs with varied strategies and 1h/1d/7d elapsed; output currency inflation, time to unlock, bottlenecks, dominant strategy, and progression dead ends. Include runnable automated test commands in README and record results.

## 17. Security, ownership, and protection
All code/art must be original or appropriately licensed; track dependencies and license compatibility. No secrets in repository, pin dependencies, validate config/save data, avoid remote code execution, do not trust client state for future multiplayer or purchases. Keep private signing keys outside repo. Name and logo are provisional: conduct trademark clearance, app-store name checks and domain checks before commercial launch. Copyright protects expression, not the general idle-gardening mechanic. Use Git remote with access controls, branch protection, backups, and contributor IP agreements if collaborators join. No guarantees against imitation; execution, distinctive visual identity, brand and distribution matter.
